Service Length:

Sunday Service: Approximately 3.5 hours (8:00 AM – 11:30 AM WAT)

Weekly Prayer Meeting (Fridays): Approximately 3 hours (5:00 PM – 8:00 PM WAT)

Monthly Prayer Contact (Last Weekend of the Month): Multi-day sessions starting Friday evening and concluding Sunday afternoon

Language: Services and teachings are conducted primarily in English.

Dress Guidance: Modest, smart-casual, or formal attire. There is no strict uniform or dress code imposed, but modest dressing aligned with Christian fellowship standards is expected.

Children's Arrangements: Children attend gatherings alongside their parents/guardians at the main worship venue, as there is no separate dedicated children's building facility at the Ado-Ekiti center.
