Directions & Venue Notes

Landmarks:   Located along Adebayo (along the main Ado–Iworoko Road axis) 

Positioned directly beside or near Stateg Mall, a well-known commercial landmark along the Adebayo stretch[cite: 1].   

Building Exterior & Description:   A prominent multi-story commercial structure along Adebayo  Features standard commercial frontage with retail shops or office spaces on the ground floor
Which Entrance & Floor: Floor: Second Floor (Top Level / Auditorium space).
Entrance: Access is via the main pedestrian stairwell along the front/side entrance of the building leading up to the second-floor worship center.
Parking & Logistics:Open street-level and perimeter parking is available in front of and surrounding the building complex.
For major weekend gatherings and monthly prayer conferences, visitors are advised to arrive early to secure convenient parking spaces along Adebayo
