Pastor (Dr.) Taiwo Omolayo serves as a resident pastor and spiritual leader at Remnant Christian Network (RCN) Ekiti, located in Ado-Ekiti.

Called with a passion for prayer, sound biblical exposition, and the spiritual maturation of believers, Pastor Taiwo is dedicated to seeing lives transformed by the Gospel and grounded in divine truth. He regularly ministers at RCN Ekiti through teaching, intercession, and shepherd-care, nurturing believers into a deeper relationship with God and equipping them to walk in their God-given destiny.

In addition to his pastoral leadership, he is a medical doctor in Ekiti State, bringing a spirit of excellence and compassionate service to both his professional vocation and ministry calling.
