Rev. Dr. Taiwo Omolayo
Background & Vocation
Rev. Dr. Taiwo Omolayo is a Consultant in Family Medicine and the Head of the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) unit at the Federal Teaching Hospital, Ido-Ekiti, Ekiti State. A dedicated medical professional, he seamlessly integrates a calling to clinical excellence with a profound commitment to Kingdom service.

Divine Calling & Ministry Journey
Dr. Omolayo’s spiritual journey is rooted in a passion for prayer, sound biblical exposition, and the spiritual awakening of believers. Called into ministry to proclaim the uncompromised Word of God, his spiritual formation aligned with the core mandate of the Remnant Christian Network (RCN)—a global ministry dedicated to striving for the rebirth of apostolic Christianity and heralding the revival of Jesus Christ.

As part of RCN's apostolic network, Dr. Omolayo was entrusted with leadership responsibility in Ekiti State. Through his leadership at RCN Ekiti, based in Ado-Ekiti, he has been instrumental in establishing an altar of prayer, discipleship, and fellowship for believers across the region.

Present Leadership at RCN Ekiti
Today, Rev. Dr. Taiwo Omolayo serves as the resident pastor and leader at RCN Ekiti. In alignment with the broader RCN vision, his ministry focuses on:

Apostolic Teaching: Delivering grounded, systematic biblical truth aimed at spiritual maturity and the empowerment of believers.

Prayer & Intercession: Raising a praying generation and sustaining spiritual altars for territorial transformation.

Empowering Destiny: Equipping believers to discover, align with, and walk in their God-given callings across all spheres of life.

Whether in the hospital ward or behind the pulpit, Dr. Omolayo operates as a dual-bearer of healing and spiritual restoration, embodying compassionate leadership, kingdom purpose, and faithful stewardship.
